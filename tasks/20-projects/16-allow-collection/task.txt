GOAL
  An OpenRouter participant of a project room fails every round with "No
  OpenRouter endpoint of <model> accepts data_collection: deny" when the model
  has no endpoint under that routing (free models often have none). Two
  changes, owner request 2026-10-24 ("do 3; if it is not set, do 1"):
  (3) a per-participant switch "Allow data collection" (default off) in the
  Room participant row; when on, that participant's request omits
  `provider.data_collection: "deny"`, everything else unchanged, and the
  "Context sent" disclosure and the recipients line say collection is allowed

  is skipped for the rest of the run: one error entry, no further calls, its
  chip shows `skipped`, relay and the turn counter pass over it; the next run

  docs/plans/projects.md D12/D13 (deny always). Serves milestone M3.
