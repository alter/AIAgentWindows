TASK: design contrast — light and dark palettes by the system setting, WCAG 2 AA and APCA, held by a gate

GOAL

  background) passes WCAG 2 AA and APCA, in a light and a dark palette that
  follow the system setting; no pair is two shades of one hue (grey text on
  black, red text on a red fill). Spoilers get the standard disclosure arrow.
