- Bracketed paste wraps pasted text in `ESC[200~ … ESC[201~` when the application enabled mode 2004; both CLIs enable it (`bracketedPasteMode=true` in `tasks/05-feasibility/01-evidence-table/spikes/claude-run.out` and `codex-run.out`) — https://github.com/xtermjs/xterm.js/blob/6.0.0/src/browser/Clipboard.ts#L23

## Theme and minimum contrast (studied for 05-design-contrast T01, 2026-10-23)

- `ITerminalOptions.theme` is an `ITheme`; fields: `foreground`, `background`, `cursor`, `cursorAccent`, `selectionBackground` ("can be transparent"), `selectionForeground`, `selectionInactiveBackground`, `scrollbarSliderBackground`, `scrollbarSliderHoverBackground`, `scrollbarSliderActiveBackground`, `overviewRulerBorder`, the sixteen ANSI names `black red green yellow blue magenta cyan white` and `brightBlack` through `brightWhite`, and `extendedAnsi` (colours 16-255). Every field is optional and falls back to the built-in default — https://github.com/xtermjs/xterm.js/blob/6.0.0/typings/xterm.d.ts#L343-L416
- Assigning `terminal.options.theme = { ... }` applies live: the `theme` option change calls `ThemeService._setTheme`, which re-parses every colour, clears the contrast caches and fires `onChangeColors`; the viewport background and the DOM renderer's stylesheet follow it. Probe on Electron 44.6.0: `scrollableElementBackground` went from `rgb(0, 0, 0)` to `rgb(255, 255, 255)` and the `.xterm-rows` rule from `color: #ffffff` to `color: #000000` — https://github.com/xtermjs/xterm.js/blob/6.0.0/src/browser/services/ThemeService.ts#L73 and https://github.com/xtermjs/xterm.js/blob/6.0.0/src/browser/services/ThemeService.ts#L81-L140 and `tasks/10-app-shell/05-design-contrast/spikes/result.txt` (EVIDENCE XT2)
- Mutating the object in place (`terminal.options.theme.background = '#00ff00'`) does nothing: the option is compared by assignment, so the probe left the background at `rgb(255, 255, 255)`. Always assign a new object — https://github.com/xtermjs/xterm.js/blob/6.0.0/src/browser/services/ThemeService.ts#L73 and `tasks/10-app-shell/05-design-contrast/spikes/result.txt` (EVIDENCE XT2)
- The terminal background is drawn on the `.xterm-scrollable-element` node (inline `background-color` from `themeService.colors.background`), not on `.xterm-viewport`; a test reads the former — https://github.com/xtermjs/xterm.js/blob/6.0.0/src/browser/Viewport.ts#L73-L74
- An opaque `selectionBackground` or `selectionInactiveBackground` is blended at 0.3 opacity over the background (issue 2737); a selection colour meant to be solid must be given with alpha — https://github.com/xtermjs/xterm.js/blob/6.0.0/src/browser/services/ThemeService.ts#L95-L107
- `minimumContrastRatio` (default 1 = do nothing; 4.5 is "Minimum for WCAG AA compliance", 21 white on black) changes the foreground of a cell when the ratio between it and its background is not met. The value is rounded to one decimal and clamped to 1..21 (probe: setting 99 read back 21, setting 0.2 read back 1) — https://github.com/xtermjs/xterm.js/blob/6.0.0/typings/xterm.d.ts#L197-L207 and https://github.com/xtermjs/xterm.js/blob/6.0.0/src/common/services/OptionsService.ts#L181-L183
- The adjustment uses the WCAG 2 ratio `(L1 + 0.05) / (L2 + 0.05)` on `relativeLuminance`: the foreground is made lighter or darker in 10 % steps until the ratio is met; if pure white or black is hit first it goes the other direction with the background colour and takes the better result; an already sufficient pair returns `undefined` (no change) — https://github.com/xtermjs/xterm.js/blob/6.0.0/src/common/Color.ts#L275-L312 and https://github.com/xtermjs/xterm.js/blob/6.0.0/src/common/Color.ts#L371-L376
- The DOM renderer applies it per cell, skips glyphs drawn as background (box drawing), and halves the ratio for dim (SGR 2) cells: with 4.5, a dim cell needs 2.25. Probe: text in `rgb(30,30,30)` on black stayed `#1e1e1e` at ratio 1 and became `#7d7d7d` after `minimumContrastRatio = 4.5` was set later, on cells already written — https://github.com/xtermjs/xterm.js/blob/6.0.0/src/browser/renderer/dom/DomRendererRowFactory.ts#L480-L497 and `tasks/10-app-shell/05-design-contrast/spikes/result.txt` (EVIDENCE XT3)
- The app's terminals use the DOM renderer (no WebGL or canvas addon is loaded; `src/renderer/terminal-pane.ts` imports `@xterm/xterm` and `@xterm/addon-fit` only), so the rules above are the ones in force — https://github.com/xtermjs/xterm.js/blob/6.0.0/src/browser/renderer/dom/DomRenderer.ts#L78-L80
